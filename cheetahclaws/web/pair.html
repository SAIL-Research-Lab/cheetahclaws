iPhone / iPad

  1. Open the Camera and point it at the QR code, then tap the link (it opens in Safari).
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen. CheetahClaws now opens like an app.

Android

  1. Scan the QR code with the camera or Google Lens and open the link in Chrome.
  2. Menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or install the CheetahClaws Android app and paste the link).
